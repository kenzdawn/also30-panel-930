Three movements

The year the way a donor will live it

Look back · Jan to Jul
30 Stories
  • 10 told by people supported, 10 by families, 10 by advocates and DSPs. Same weight, same format, one a week for thirty weeks. Story 30 is told live at the Expedition Summit in July.
  • The first ten already exist on ALSO's blog (Trey, Holly, Skyler, Glenda, Miranda's 28 years); the campaign gives them a frame, consent on file, and an ask. The first Montage resident tells a story the week the building opens.
  • Each story is tagged to a pillar and ends with one concrete, priced way to help.
  • Consent protocol built with the person, not for them: plain-language agreement, a named story owner, a standing right to change or withdraw.
  • Every story becomes a reusable asset (web page, 60-second video, social card, email). This builds the storytelling library in plan section 6.
  • Heart Work Virtual Tour gets a 30th-anniversary edition each month, hosted by that month's storyteller where they choose to.
Look around · July
Expedition: Bridge to Inclusion, 30th Edition
  • Keep what worked in 2024: the TriMet-presented urban team challenge, Disability Pride Month timing, the Summit Celebration. Plan for 30 teams (120+ people), which clears the plan's 100+ goal.
  • Answer why 2025 was paused: built to net, not to spend. Sponsorship sold first, course and venue in-kind, costs capped before registration opens.
  • Founders' Summit: Story 30 told live, three storytellers paired with the pillar their story funds, 1997 founders and early board and staff as honored guests. A live "fund a pillar" thermometer in the room and on this page.
  • Regional relays: one-hour satellite challenges in Bend and Klamath Falls the same morning, hosted by chamber partners, so the 30th belongs to all nine counties.
  • Illustrative net: sponsorships $22,500 + teams and Summit $12,000 + live giving $7,500, less $9,500 capped costs = about $32,500.
Look forward · Sep to Dec
The Next Thirty
  • DSP Appreciation Week (Sept 7 to 13) opens the Team Builders Fund with the advocates' own stories.
  • Heart Work Circle: ALSO's Access 10 program grows a $30-a-month level; every Access 10 member is invited to step up. Pushed through GivingTuesday (Nov 30) and year-end. Target 100 members, about $36,000 a year recurring. Monthly donors retain near 80%; first-time donors, 19%.
  • Legacy of Belonging: ALSO's existing Legacy Partner option, relaunched for the 30th, two years ahead of the plan's Phase 3. A one-page invitation to founders, long-tenured staff, and 10-year-plus donors. No hard asks.
  • Year-end appeal: Story 31 is written by the reader. Folds in the Children's Holiday Wish List and the HEARTS raffle so three asks become one voice.

Risks and asks

What could go wrong, and what I would need

Risks

  • One-person department. Fixed weekly cadence, story production batched in Q4 2026, staff ambassadors.
  • Story consent or dignity failure. Written protocol, named story owners, review by someone supported before publishing.
  • The event eats the year, or repeats 2025. Sponsorship sold before registration opens, costs capped, committee of former stewardship team and board, date and course locked by March.
  • CRM not ready. Audit is the first Q4 task; launch does not move for it, but tracking does.

What I would ask of ALSO

  • An executive sponsor for the campaign and a board champion for 30 Partners.
  • Ten hours a month from Programs to price pillar units and introduce storytellers.
  • A small production budget for video and print, offset by in-kind asks to partners.
  • Permission to set targets above the plan and report honestly if we miss them.
  • Access to alumni and current sponsors: 1997 founders, former board and staff, TriMet, Key Bank, the 70+ employers. They are the first thirty calls.
