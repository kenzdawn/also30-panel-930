Thirty Years of Belonging

Heart work built this community. Your support keeps it beating.

301997 – 2027

Since 1997, ALSO (Advocates for Life Skills & Opportunity) has helped Oregonians with intellectual and developmental disabilities live, work, and belong in their own communities.

This concept turns the 30th anniversary into a year of stories, one gathering, and a way to keep giving. Here is what a supporter would receive, one envelope at a time.

Three envelopes. One year.

Open each one to see what lands in a supporter's inbox.

Story 1 of 30: “I wanted to leave when I wanted to.”

Thirty years ago, Oregon families decided that people with developmental disabilities should live in their own communities, on their own terms. This year we are telling thirty stories about what that made possible, one a week.

This is the first. It is about a ramp, and about what a ramp is really for.
